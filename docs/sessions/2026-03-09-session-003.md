## POC Results Integrated

The Local LLM POC validated the following:

| Question | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **OQ-01**: Local LLM backend | **Ollama** — 9x faster throughput vs vLLM, 4x less VRAM via Q4 quantization, native OpenAI-compatible API, clean lifecycle management |
| **OQ-08**: WSL2 + RTX 5090 | **Fully compatible** — works out of the box, no CUDA toolkit install needed in WSL2 |

### Key Benchmarks (Ollama, RTX 5090 32 GB)

| Model | Params | VRAM | Throughput | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| qwen2.5-coder:7b | 7B | 6.8 GB | 232 tok/s | Fast, good for iteration |
| codestral:22b | 22B | 19.4 GB | 95 tok/s | **Recommended** — best quality/speed tradeoff |
| qwen2.5:72b | 72B | 23.7 GB | 1.9 tok/s | Too slow for iterative evolution |

### vLLM Comparison

- 7B model: 26 tok/s (vs Ollama's 232 tok/s) with 28.1 GB VRAM (vs 6.8 GB)
- 22B model: OOM on 32 GB GPU (fp16 only)
- Verdict: not recommended for V1 single-user local use case
